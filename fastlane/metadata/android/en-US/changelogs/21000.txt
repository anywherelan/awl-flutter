Devices can connect directly on the same Wi-Fi. awl connects to the first known device up to 4 seconds sooner after startup.

New devices get a VPN IP from their ID. Large UDP packets now get through, and the VPN uses up to 7% less CPU.

Some phones used mobile data to answer discovery requests. awl skips those replies by default.

New installs use MTU 1400. Older installs keep 3500 on upgrade. Change vpn.mtu on either the older or newer installs so all your devices use the same MTU.
